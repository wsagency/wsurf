# WSurf — “Horizon W” (opus-5-5)

**Idea.** A single disc cut by one hard-edged, equal-angle zigzag. The cut makes the disc a sun setting over the sea, and the gap itself is the hidden W: the moment the surf line meets the horizon.

**Strength.** It has one compact, unmistakable silhouette. The W gap still reads at 16 px in colour, monochrome positive and reverse.

**Trade-off.** Sun-over-zigzag is a familiar outdoor/sunset trope, so the mark depends on its exact proportions to stay ownable. At ≤24 px, use `-small.svg` (wider gap).

**Clear space.** At least ¼ of the mark width on every side.

**Colours.** Sun: #FFC14A → #FF5530. Sea: #3B6CFF → #1C2FBF. Themed file: `--c1..--c4` in that order. For monochrome, set all four to one colour.

**Provenance.** Authored from scratch by Claude Opus 5.5 (anthropic/claude-opus-5-5) for this task. Geometry was computed with a throwaway Python script and rendered with AppKit/WebKit. No stock or third-party artwork; no Apple HIG template used.
