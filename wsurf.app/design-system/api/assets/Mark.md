## Usage notes

The Tandem mark. Two flat discs; lead `#3B1FD1` / `#6D55FF` (`mark-lead`), follower `#14C79A` / `#2FE0B2` (`mark-follower`).

- `mark-light.svg`, `mark-dark.svg`: 1024 square master, optically centred. Day and Night inks.
- `mark.svg`: the same, colours from `--wsurf-lead` and `--wsurf-follower` (Day fallbacks). Inline it to theme it.
- `mark-tight-light.svg`, `mark-tight-dark.svg`, `mark-tight.svg`: cropped to the drawn shape plus 2% (viewBox `96.82 142.77 815.36 727.96`), for lockups and layouts.
- `mark-small-light.svg`, `mark-small-dark.svg`, `mark-small.svg`: the small cut for 24px and below (bigger follower, wider gap, no fillets).
