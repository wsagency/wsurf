## Usage notes

The previous mark draft (`branding/` in `wsagency/wsurf`, PR #24), kept for historical reference after Tandem was chosen. Do not use it in new work.

- `mark-flux-light.svg`, `mark-flux-dark.svg`, `lockup-flux-light.svg`, `lockup-flux-dark.svg`: the switch mark and its Figtree Bold lockup (Figtree outlines: Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1).
- `mark-ember-*`, `mark-tide-*`, `mark-grove-*`, `mark-bloom-*`, `mark-graphite-*`: the five other colour palettes drawn on the superseded Flux mark. Their colour tokens remain in `tokens.json` and `tokens.css` for reference only; the current app Dock icon finishes are Default (Anodized), Anodized, Steel, Copper, Light, Dark, and Mono (see `../../assets/Dock/README.md` and `WSurf/Settings/DockIcon.swift`).
